Stay safe online with these tips

⚠

What is cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is when someone uses the internet to hurt, scare, or embarrass another person. If this is happening to you, tap Report Harm.

›
🔒

Keeping your information private

Never share your full name, home address, phone number, or school name with strangers online. Use nicknames on social media.

›
👥

Online friends and strangers

Not everyone online is who they say they are. Be careful about meeting online friends in person. Talk to a trusted adult first.

›
🚧

Digital boundaries

You have the right to say no. If someone pressures you to do something online that makes you uncomfortable, that is not okay. Report it.

›
🆘

How to get help

If something bad happens online, you can: 1) Tell a trusted adult. 2) Use GuardianLink to report it. 3) Call 116 (Child Helpline). You are not alone.
